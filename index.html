9-Stage Pre-Publication Governance Architecture

Complete lifecycle from ingestion to autonomous audit trail (BrandWise Solution Proposal Section 02).

Stage 1
Multi-Channel Content Ingestion Copy paste, file upload (PDF/DOCX), and CMS webhook connectors.
Stage 2
Tokenization & Linguistic Normalization Contextual Indian English fairness ('prepone', 'revert back' not penalized).
Stage 3
Deterministic Rule Matching RegEx checks for brand casing, prohibited vocabulary, and mandatory legal disclaimers.
Stage 4
Semantic Alignment & Claim Ledger Claim verification against CLM-001..CLM-006; outputs 'cannot verify - needs human check' if expired.
Stage 5
Visual & Accessibility Studio WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA color contrast ratio calculator and Flesch-Kincaid reading ease scores.
Stage 6
Composite Weighted Scoring Claims (35%), Brand Voice (25%), Legal (25%), Visual (15%) with Lenient/Balanced/Strict modes.
Stage 7
Auto-Resolution & One-Click Fixes Suggested edits (e.g. 'up to 20%') applied instantly with revision creation.
Stage 8
Collaborative Review & Version Diff Side-by-side comparison, reviewer annotations, and 6-state governance lifecycle.
Stage 9
Audit Trail & Ambiguous Guideline Detection Full changelog, dispute rate analytics, and agency compliance benchmarks.
