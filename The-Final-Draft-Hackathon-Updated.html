Prototype Review Flow

Implemented workflow and current prototype boundaries.

Stage 1
Text input and supported imports Paste text or import TXT, Markdown, CSV, PDF, or DOCX. Google Docs, Slides, and CMS connectors are not implemented.
Stage 2
Text preparation HTML tags and required-disclaimer text are handled for readability checks. Hindi and regional-language validation are not implemented.
Stage 3
Deterministic checks Rule-based checks use the current claims ledger and guidelines for prohibited wording, claims, disclaimer presence, and text accessibility.
Stage 4
Claim ledger review Checks active status and expiry. Optional Gemini citations are checked for text relevance; uncertain items are sent for human review.
Stage 5
Accessibility utilities Text reading grade, heading order, and a manual color-contrast calculator. Artwork and logo pixels are not analyzed.
Stage 6
Severity-based score Findings deduct points according to Lenient, Balanced, or Strict settings. This is a heuristic score, not a calibrated accuracy or legal-compliance probability.
Stage 7
Suggested fixes A person may apply or edit a suggested replacement; accepted changes create a revision.
Stage 8
Review and approval Revisions, comments, and role-checked review stages. Approval requires the configured threshold and no pending critical/high finding; publication requires approval and all findings dispositioned.
Stage 9
Stored review history Actions are recorded on audit records. Insights uses non-sample saved records plus a synthetic labeled ruleset; it is not an immutable audit log or production benchmark.

Not implemented: OCR, image/logo review, Google Docs/Canva/Figma, Hindi/regional-language support, and SSO.
